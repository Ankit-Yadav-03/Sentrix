# OSHA_REPORT_ID: 202-0427-33
# GROUND_TRUTH: FFH.02
# SITE_ID: OIL_SITE_11

OSHA Near-Miss Incident Report

Incident Date: 2026-08-12
Location: Utility Area, Cooling Tower CT-3
Reported by: Maintenance Foreman

Summary:
A maintenance technician was descending from Cooling Tower CT-3 using a portable extension ladder when the ladder foot slipped on a wet concrete surface at the base of the tower. The ladder slipped laterally approximately 300mm before the technician caught the ladder rails. The technician did not fall but experienced a significant near-loss of balance at approximately 3.5 metres elevation. No injury occurred.

The concrete pad had standing water from cooling tower blowdown overflow. The ladder was set up on the wet surface without a ladder mat or securing system. The base of the ladder was not footed by a second person as required by site ladder safety procedure SOP-MAINT-012 for any ladder used above 3 metres. The ladder foot pads were worn, providing reduced grip. The maintenance team was understaffed on the shift.

Equipment: Extension ladder, portable ladder.
Activity: Maintenance.
